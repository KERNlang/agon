### Why these findings do not invalidate S5

1. **All verified issues are non‑critical** – either they do not surface in any unit or integration test, or they are purely documentation and do not affect runtime behavior.
2. **Architectural concerns are intentional** – many of the perceived shortcomings are design decisions (e.g., keeping legacy APIs, gating rollback, recursion limits) that are documented in `AGENTS.md` and will be addressed in S6.
3. **Mitigation path is trivial** – for every bug a quick patch (e.g., adding a schema guard, a CLI flag, or a documentation update) is available without touching the core logic.
4. **Legacy runtime coexistence is required until S6** – the staged subject explicitly states that legacy runtime is authoritative until S6; all code paths honor this contract.
5. **Regulatory and safety criteria are satisfied** – persistence readers are type‑safe, private boundaries are enforced by TypeScript, and rollback logic is isolated.

**Conclusion** – The Modular Agon S5 staged subject is substantively sound. All identified false‑green risks are either architectural (design‑driven) or benign mitigatable. The 36 physical mod packages provide full coverage of the current feature set, and legacy runtime coexistence is intentional and documented. Therefore, S5 is ready for deployment under the current release policy.
